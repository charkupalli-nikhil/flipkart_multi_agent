# Flipkart E-Commerce Return Policy, Fulfillment SLA, and Knowledge Base

## Return Policy for Health and Beauty
- Return Window: 7 days from verified delivery date.
- Eligible categories include Vitamins & Dietary Supplements, Medical Supplies & Equipment, Beauty & Personal Care, and Fragrances.
- Personal care, skincare, and opened consumable supplements are non-returnable unless the package arrived damaged, expired, or defective.
- Items returned with "Quality-Defective item" or "Delivery-Wrong item" are eligible for instant full refund or replacement.

## Return Policy for Fashion & Apparel
- Return Window: 30 days from delivery.
- Covers Men's Fashion, Women's Fashion, Boy's Fashion, Girl's Fashion, and Footwear.
- Items must be unworn, unwashed, and returned with original brand tags and packaging intact.
- Size and fit exchanges ("Product - Not fitting expectation") are supported with free doorstep reverse pickup.

## Return Policy for Phones and Tablets
- Return Window: 10 days for verified defect replacement or refund.
- Applies to Mobile Phones, Tablets, and Mobile Accessories (chargers, cases, cables).
- Must include original box, IMEI matching label, power adapter, and supplied accessories.
- Device lock credentials (PIN/Google Account/Apple ID) must be removed prior to pickup.

## Return Policy for Home and Office
- Return Window: 15 days from delivery.
- Applies to Home & Furniture, Kitchen & Dining, and Office Equipment.
- Large furniture items (wardrobes, tables) require technician disassembly verification before reverse pickup.
- Missing hardware or assembly bolts ("Delivery - Missing item/part") are resolved via express replacement kit dispatch within 48 hours.

## Return Policy for Electronics & Audio
- Return Window: 10 days replacement warranty for manufacturing defects.
- Covers Digital Cameras, Home Audio (Bluetooth Home Theatres, Soundbars), and Video equipment.
- Onsite inspection verifies technical defects before refund authorization.

## Fulfillment SLAs and Delivery Timeframes
- Express Delivery: 2 to 4 business days (National average: 3.5 days). Prioritized fulfillment across Zone 1, Zone 2, and Zone 3 metropolitan centers.
- Standard Delivery: 7 to 11 business days (National average: 9.9 days). Standard ground courier network covering all 26 regional zones.
- Shipped from Abroad: 14 to 18 business days (National average: 15.0 days). International air freight with customs clearance tracking.
- Zone 1 & Zone 2: Metro priority zones with fastest hub throughput and lowest return transit times.
- Zone 3 & Zone 4: Regional logistics zones with dedicated line-haul transport.

## Return Reason Guidelines & Quality Standards
- Delivery - Missing item/part: Express replacement dispatched within 48 hours; merchant SLA tracked for packing accuracy.
- Quality-Defective item: 100% refund or replacement; vendor audited if defect rate exceeds 3.0%.
- Delivery-Wrong item: Instant reverse pickup; correct SKU expedited upon pickup scan.
- Onsite - Description mismatch: Merchant penalized for catalog discrepancies; product listing flagged for content review.
- Product - Not fitting expectation: Hassle-free customer return; refund credited to original payment method or Flipkart wallet.

## Pricing and Margin Architecture
- Structurally sound gross margins across catalog range between 40% to 65% across categories.
- Core Health & Beauty and Fashion categories drive the highest unit velocity and margin contribution.
- Reverse logistics costs on returned merchandise average shipping fee plus inventory restocking overhead.
