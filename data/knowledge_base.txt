# Flipkart Return Policy and Knowledge Base

## Return Policy for Electronics
- Electronics can be returned within 14 days of purchase.
- The item must be in its original packaging.
- Damaged electronics will not be accepted unless reported within 24 hours of delivery.

## Return Policy for Clothing
- Clothing can be returned within 30 days of purchase.
- Items must be unworn and unwashed with original tags attached.

## Return Policy for Books
- Books can be returned within 7 days of purchase.
- E-books are non-refundable once downloaded.

## Discount Guidelines
- Discounts over 25% require manager approval.
- The "Big Billion Days" sale allows discounts up to 40% on select categories without manager approval.
- Employee discounts are strictly 15% and cannot be combined with other offers.

## Delivery Timeframes
- North Region: 2-4 business days.
- South Region: 3-5 business days.
- East Region: 4-7 business days.
- West Region: 3-5 business days.
